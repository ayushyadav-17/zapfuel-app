## Brand & Style
This design system fuels a hyper-energized, unapologetic protein snack and shake experience engineered for Gen Z digital natives. The brand voice is irreverent, electric, and pulse-raising—marrying the raw impact of neo-brutalism with dopamine-infused cyber-pop aesthetics.

Key principles:
- **Maximum Dopamine:** High-saturation clashes, glowing fluorescent edges, and playful kinetic tension replace sterile wellness neutrals.
- **Neo-Brutalist Rigor:** Offset chunky drop-shadows (hard, unblurred 100% opacity depth), heavy structural black outlines, and sticker-style UI badges.
- **Tactile Hyper-Physicality:** Interactive elements mimic tactile candy packaging, street stickers, and gaming arcade triggers. Interfaces feel punchy, fast, and intensely reactive.

## Colors
The palette operates primarily in an immersive, pitch-obsidian dark space (`#111116`) to let high-voltage fluorescent shades burst with maximum luminescence. 

- **Primary (`#CCFF00` - Voltage Lime):** Primary call-to-actions, hero badges, high-impact key performance callouts, and hyperactive accents.
- **Secondary (`#FF007F` - Cyber Magenta):** Flavor markers, energetic tags, active states, and contrast accents.
- **Tertiary (`#2E29FF` - Cobalt Surge):** Structural interactive borders, secondary triggers, tech callouts, and electric base containers.
- **Supporting Accents:** Sunny Yellow (`#FFE600`) for limited drops and punchy highlights; Hyper Violet (`#8A2BE2`) for depth-level card backdrops and gradients.
- **Neutral Surface & Obsidian (`#111116`):** The canvas floor. Structural borders consistently deploy solid `#111116` when layered over neon fills, and pure `#CCFF00` or `#FF007F` when slicing against dark backgrounds.

## Typography
Typographic scale leverages stark contrast between expressive display weight and technical legibility.

- **Headlines & Display (Syne):** Sculptural, punchy, wide, and aggressive. Used for hero claims, product flavor titles, macro nutrition callouts, and section breaks. Always set tightly with slight negative tracking.
- **Body & Continuous Copy (Chivo):** Crisp, geometric grotesque with athletic velocity. Maintains legibility in tight specs, ingredients, and nutrition facts without losing edge.
- **Labels & System Tags (Space Grotesk):** Monospaced-leaning, high-precision technical feel. Rendered in all-caps for sticker badges, protein counters, and button text.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows an elastic 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop and tablet, collapsing to a high-density 4-column system on mobile devices.

- **Breakpoints:** Mobile (320px–767px), Tablet (768px–1023px), Desktop (1024px+).
- **Density:** Spacing relies on compact rhythm (`space-sm` to `space-md`) within component clusters to keep interfaces arcade-dense, while utilizing sudden hyper-scale gaps (`space-2xl`) between promotional blocks for dynamic pacing.
- **Overlapping Planes:** Key assets (bottle renders, protein bars, nutritional badges) break container boundaries and bleed over column gutters by `0.5rem` to `1rem` to create deliberate compositional collision.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth eschews realistic diffused ambient shadows in favor of neo-brutalist hard-offset drop shadows and hyper-saturated inner glows:

- **Hard-Drop Depth (Neo-Brutalist Offset):** Cards, primary buttons, and floating panels feature a 0px blur, 100% opacity offset shadow (`4px 4px 0px #000000` or `#CCFF00` on dark canvas). Active states translate by `2px 2px` towards the anchor with shadow collapsing to `2px 2px 0px`.
- **Cyber Glow Highlighting:** Critical interactive triggers incorporate high-voltage edge halos using low-spread, saturated box-shadows (e.g., `0px 0px 16px rgba(204, 255, 0, 0.4)`).
- **Z-Index Layering:** Stickers, floating macro stamps (e.g., "30G PROTEIN"), and flash sale tags sit atop primary surfaces with rotation transforms (`-3deg` to `+4deg`) to amplify depth and tactile play.

## Shapes
Shapes strike a dynamic equilibrium between heavy rounded containers (`0.5rem` base, `1rem` container corners) and razor-sharp accent geometry.

- **Containers & Surface Cards:** Standardized at `0.5rem` (`rounded`) to provide structure while housing sticker components.
- **Pill Badges & Stickers:** Fully rounded (`9999px` / pill-shaped) for micro-metrics, calorie tags, and filter pills.
- **Structural Outlines:** Surfaces maintain an explicit, solid 2px or 3px stroke—grounding the candy-coated colors with graphic-novel definition.

## Components

- **Buttons:** 
  - *Primary ("Turbo"):* Voltage Lime (`#CCFF00`) fill, obsidian (`#111116`) text and 2.5px border, hard offset shadow (`4px 4px 0px #111116` on light; `4px 4px 0px #FF007F` on dark). Space Grotesk uppercase label.
  - *Secondary:* Transparent canvas with 2.5px solid neon border, pure white or neon text, active state shifts to fill.
  - *Interaction:* On press, translate `translate(2px, 2px)` with shadow reduced to 2px offset.
- **Sticker Badges & Macro Pills:**
  - Pill-shaped tags (`9999px` radius) with high-contrast fills (e.g., Punchy Pink `#FF007F` with White text, or Voltage Lime `#CCFF00` with Dark text).
  - Skewed `-2deg` or `+3deg` in hero product compositions to mimic physical product decals.
- **Cards (Snack & Shake Display):**
  - Dark obsidian background (`#16161D`) bounded by a 2px stroke in Cobalt Blue (`#2E29FF`) or Voltage Lime.
  - Hard drop shadow (`6px 6px 0px #000000`). Product packaging floats outside the top card boundary.
  - Top-right corner houses dynamic floating pill counters for grams of protein and BCAAs.
- **Input Fields:**
  - Heavy 2px solid border (`#2E29FF`), dark surface fill, high-contrast neon focus states with an inner glow. Placeholder copy in muted lilac gray.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:**
  - Chunky custom squares/circles with a 2px black border, solid Voltage Lime fill when checked, populated by a thick, razor-edged checkmark glyph.
- **Flavor Selector Chips:**
  - Segmented pill chips with colored thumbnail dot indicator; selected state pops with full-color saturation and a 2px offset shadow.
- **Nutrition Metric HUD (Custom Component):**
  - Ultra-condensed technical data tiles showcasing Protein, Carbs, Calories, and Sugar with high-contrast vertical dividers, styled like racing telemetry or arcade stat counters.
